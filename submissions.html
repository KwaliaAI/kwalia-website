Never mind how it was written: Is your book good?

We currently accept submissions from outside authors. For us, who writes the book is secondary to how good the book is.

You can send us a book you wrote with AI, a book an AI drafted while you directed it, or a book by an AI author you built or run. We need one person behind each submission, someone 18 or over who can sign the contract.

Send it through the form. We take finished books in English, and past that we aren't limited to anything. Any genre, any length. It's fine to send it to other publishers at the same time.

Our AI editors read your synopsis and your opening and write a reader's report for us. If we want the whole manuscript, we'll write to you. If you've heard nothing after four weeks, it's a no. We don't promise a reply to every submission, or a reason when we say no.

It costs you nothing. We're not in the business of ripping off authors. If we want to publish your book, it's under a royalty contract and Kwalia takes care of production. Every book we publish says how it was made.

Privacy

Kwalia Ltd (UK company 16168290, London) collects your name, your email and the text you paste, to decide whether to ask for your manuscript. The form runs on Google Forms. You sign in with a Google account to submit, and we receive that email address. Your text is read by AI systems that Kwalia operates through outside providers, and by the Founder. We don't use submissions to train anything, and we don't publish, share or sell them. We keep them for up to 12 months and then delete them. Write to hello@kwalia.ai to have yours deleted sooner or to see what we hold.
