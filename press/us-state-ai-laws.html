Kwalia Books publishes US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes

Kwalia Books has added US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes to its AI Law & Governance Library. The 2026 edition brings together nine legislative texts from California, Colorado, Texas, Utah and New York.

The volume includes California’s Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act, Colorado’s 2026 legislation on automated decisions, conversational services and psychotherapy, the Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act, three Utah Code chapters and New York’s 2026 RAISE Act amendment. The full list appears on the back cover and in the contents.

The official source texts were retrieved on 9 November 2026. The edition identifies enacted provisions with future operative dates.

US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes is Volume 5 of the AI Law & Governance Library, following The EU AI Act, Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights, The NIST AI Risk Management Framework and China’s AI Regulations.

The AI Law & Governance Library collects primary laws and governance texts on artificial intelligence in reference editions published by Kwalia Books.

Kwalia Books publica US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes

Kwalia Books incorpora US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes a la AI Law & Governance Library. La edición de 2026 reúne nueve textos legislativos de California, Colorado, Texas, Utah y Nueva York.

El volumen incluye la Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act de California, tres leyes de Colorado aprobadas en 2026 sobre decisiones automatizadas, servicios conversacionales y psicoterapia, la Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act, tres capítulos del Código de Utah y la modificación de 2026 de la RAISE Act de Nueva York. La contracubierta y el índice enumeran todos los textos incluidos.

Los textos oficiales se obtuvieron el 9 de noviembre de 2026. La edición identifica las disposiciones aprobadas cuya aplicación comienza en una fecha posterior. Los textos legislativos se reproducen en inglés.

US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes es el volumen 5 de la AI Law & Governance Library, después de The EU AI Act, Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights, The NIST AI Risk Management Framework y China’s AI Regulations.
