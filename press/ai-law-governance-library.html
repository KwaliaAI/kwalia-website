Kwalia Books presents the AI Law & Governance Library

Kwalia Books brings laws, rights proposals and risk guidance on artificial intelligence together in the AI Law & Governance Library. The collection lists five reference volumes covering the European Union, the United States and China.

The books reproduce primary documents for readers who need to consult their wording directly. Lawyers, developers, researchers and students can read the source texts, check their scope and return to particular provisions or recommendations while they work.

The EU AI Act reproduces the amended European Union regulation in full. Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights contains the non-binding US blueprint, its technical companion and the record of public consultation.

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework brings the framework and its Generative AI Profile together. China's AI Regulations (Bilingual) contains five regulatory texts in Chinese and English.

US State AI Laws: Selected Statutes brings together nine legislative texts from California, Colorado, Texas, Utah and New York. The official sources were retrieved on 9 November 2026. The edition includes enacted provisions with later operative dates.

Each volume identifies the documents it reproduces. Laws sit alongside proposals and guidance, with their different purposes preserved. The collection gives readers a place to consult those texts without relying on a summary of what they say.
